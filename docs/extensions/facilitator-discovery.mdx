---
title: "Facilitator Discovery"
description: "Publish one JSON document so clients, servers, and other facilitators can find your facilitator, its endpoints, and its public receipts."
---

The `facilitator-discovery` extension gives every facilitator a
machine-readable home page: a JSON descriptor at
`/.well-known/x402-facilitator` that advertises who operates the
facilitator, which networks and schemes it supports, where its
verify/settle endpoints live, and where its public receipts and activity
pulse can be read.

Resource discovery (the [Bazaar](/extensions/bazaar)) answers "who sells
what?". Facilitator discovery answers the complementary question: "who
can settle this payment, and how do I talk to them?" Protocol details —
field definitions, the registry endpoints, federation semantics, and
receipt formats — live in the
[extension specification](https://github.com/x402-foundation/x402/blob/main/specs/extensions/facilitator-discovery.md).

### Example descriptor

From the reference deployment at
https://musebook.trade/.well-known/x402-facilitator:

```json
{
  "x402m": "v0",
  "x402Version": 2,
  "id": "musebook",
  "operator": "clawd",
  "endpoints": {
    "supported": "https://musebook.trade/api/x402/supported",
    "pricing": "https://musebook.trade/api/x402/pricing",
    "verify": "https://musebook.trade/api/x402/verify",
    "settle": "https://musebook.trade/api/x402/settle",
    "receipts": "https://musebook.trade/api/x402/receipts",
    "pulse": "https://musebook.trade/api/x402/pulse",
    "facilitators": "https://musebook.trade/api/x402/facilitators"
  },
  "networks": [
    "solana:5eykt4UsFv8P8NJdTREpY1vzqKqZKvdp",
    "solana:EtWTRABZaYq6iMfeYKouRu166VU2xqa1",
    "eip155:8453",
    "eip155:84532"
  ],
  "extensions": ["x402m-batch-v0", "x402m-receipts-v0", "x402m-referral-v0"]
}
```

### Using a descriptor

1. Fetch `/.well-known/x402-facilitator` from the facilitator's origin.
2. Cross-check the advertised networks and schemes against the
   facilitator's `/supported` response — discovery is an advertisement,
   not a trust decision.
3. Read public receipts and the activity pulse to sanity-check that the
   facilitator actually settles what it advertises.

Facilitators can also register in a peer registry (`GET`/`POST` on the
`facilitators` endpoint in the descriptor) so clients can enumerate
known facilitators instead of hardcoding one; settlements that pass
through more than one facilitator identify the originator on their
receipts.
